Verify means to check that something is true or correct.


Plan means a list of steps to reach a goal.


Memory means stored information that can be recalled later.


Policy means a rule that decides what is allowed.


Tool means an instrument used to perform a task.


Skill means a validated reusable procedure.


Evidence means observed facts that support a claim.


Risk means the chance of an unwanted outcome.


Goal means a desired future state.


Task means a unit of work with a clear end.


Agent means a system that acts toward goals.


Model means a mathematical description learned from data.


Token means a piece of text used by a language model.


Epoch means one full pass over training data.


Batch means a group of examples processed together.
